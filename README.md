# ProductIntelligence

# 🚀 Product Intelligence AI

Product Intelligence AI is a web-based platform that helps users upload, analyze, validate, and generate reports from product datasets.

It automatically identifies product information, detects data-quality issues, calculates product quality scores, and provides clear validation and intelligence reports.

## ✨ Features

- 📊 Interactive Dashboard
- 📁 Upload CSV, Excel, JSON and TSV datasets
- 🛍️ Automatic Product Detection
- ✅ Product Data Validation
- ⚠️ Missing Data & Duplicate Detection
- 📈 Product Quality Score
- 📑 Overall & Individual Reports
- 🌓 Light/Dark Mode
- 📱 Responsive UI
- ⚡ FastAPI Backend
- 🗄️ SQLite/PostgreSQL Database

## 🔄 How It Works

1. Upload a product dataset.
2. The system analyzes the dataset automatically.
3. Product records are detected dynamically.
4. Product information is validated.
5. Missing fields, duplicates and data-quality issues are identified.
6. Quality scores are calculated.
7. Overall and individual product reports are generated.
